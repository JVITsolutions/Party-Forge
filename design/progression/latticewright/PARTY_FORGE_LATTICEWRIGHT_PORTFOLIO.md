# Party Forge Latticewright progression portfolio

This directory contains 16 independently editable format-3 projects and deterministic runtime-v3 design exports: the City, nine established classes, and six established buildings. Native portals form a closed City-to-destination-and-return network.

The files are authoring/design data. Party Forge gameplay code still consumes its separate fixed City fixture; class/building effects here are intentionally empty until their contracts and balance are approved. No old graph was migrated or overwritten.
